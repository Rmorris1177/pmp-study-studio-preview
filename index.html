Content basis: supplied lesson material and the new outline, supplemented with public PMI, ASQ and Scrum Guide references. Full course text for Lessons 6–12 was not supplied. Those lessons follow the supplied titles and public references. Older PMI articles support enduring techniques; the July 2026 outline controls exam alignment.

Coverage limits: this is a learning preview with balanced lesson practice, not an exam-weighted mock assessment. It introduces each listed topic; advanced contract calculations, detailed scheduling networks, simulation-based risk analysis, and deeper AI and sustainability cases need additional practice. Independent subject-matter review and learner testing remain necessary before a commercial release. Practice scores have not been validated as readiness predictions.
